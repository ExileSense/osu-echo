Fills in the profile above (picture, banner, bio, details; click Save changes afterwards), adds your best scores and first place ranks (no replays) and, if ticked, looks up your official leaderboard rank on each top play. Needs your own osu! OAuth client in .data/osu_oauth.json. Takes about a minute, or a few minutes with the leaderboard lookup, because requests are paced for osu!'s rate limit. Safe to repeat.
